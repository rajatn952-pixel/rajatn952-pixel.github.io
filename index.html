About the Author

Rajat Mishra is an Indian author and writer whose work explores timeless stories and themes from Indian literature through a contemporary storytelling approach.

His debut work, The Eternal Ramayana: Book I of VI — Bala Kanda, begins a six-part retelling inspired by the Ramayana of Maharishi Valmiki, bringing the epic's enduring characters, events and values to contemporary readers.
